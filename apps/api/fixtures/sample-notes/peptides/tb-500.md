# TB-500

TB-500 is a synthetic fragment of thymosin beta-4, discussed in the sample
notes for muscle recovery, flexibility, and reducing inflammation after
training load.

## Dosing

The sample notes describe a loading phase of 2–2.5 mg twice weekly for 4–6
weeks, followed by a maintenance dose of 2 mg once weekly. Subcutaneous
injection is the only route in the sample notes.

## Stacking

The sample notes mention TB-500 alongside [[BPC-157]] for injury recovery,
describing the pair as complementary: TB-500 systemic, BPC-157 more local to
the injury site.

## Safety

Sample-note cautions: theoretical concerns around cell migration mean the
sample notes advise against use with any history of malignancy, and emphasize
sourcing quality given the research-chemical market.
