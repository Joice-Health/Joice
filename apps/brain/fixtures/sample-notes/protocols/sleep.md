# Sleep support protocol

The sample notes group sleep support around three levers: circadian timing,
GABAergic support, and growth-hormone-adjacent peptides.

## DSIP

Delta sleep-inducing peptide appears in the sample notes at 100–250 mcg
subcutaneously, 30–60 minutes before bed, described as most useful for
sleep-onset trouble rather than sleep maintenance.

## Non-peptide basics first

The sample notes are explicit: no peptide protocol before the basics — a
fixed wake time, morning light exposure, caffeine cutoff 8 hours before bed,
and a cool, dark room. Peptides are described as the last 10%, not the
foundation.
