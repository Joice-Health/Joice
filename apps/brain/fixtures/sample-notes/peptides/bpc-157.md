# BPC-157

Body Protection Compound 157 is a synthetic pentadecapeptide derived from a
protective protein found in gastric juice. In the sample literature it is
discussed for soft-tissue repair, tendon healing, and gut-lining support.

## Dosing

Sample research protocols describe 250–500 mcg once or twice daily. Both
subcutaneous injection near the affected area and oral capsules appear in the
sample notes; oral dosing is described as preferable for gut-focused goals.

### Cycling

The sample notes describe 4–8 week cycles followed by an equal break, with
reassessment before any repeat cycle.

## Safety

Described in the sample notes as generally well tolerated in short studies,
with the caveat that long-term human data is limited. Flagged interactions:
none noted, but the sample notes advise disclosing all peptide use to a
clinician.
